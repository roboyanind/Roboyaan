Three areas, taught hands-on

Each workshop is built around making something. Topics below are what we typically cover; we adjust depth and pace to the class or year group.

AI and Machine Learning

Students learn the complete pipeline, from writing their first Python scripts to fine-tuning and deploying real AI applications on standard laptops.

  • From Python basics to advanced data handling
  • Training and fine-tuning custom AI models
  • Deploying completely local, offline LLMs
  • Integrating AI into custom web and app builds

Electronics and Robotics

Students wire real circuits, program microcontrollers and see their code move something in the physical world.

  • Circuits, sensors and Arduino boards
  • Basic soldering with supervision
  • Building and programming a small robot
  • Intro to embedded electronics and IoT

Drone Technology

Students learn how drones fly, what the parts do and how they are controlled, with flying done safely and within local rules.

  • How multirotor drones work
  • Parts: motors, flight controller, battery
  • Flight basics and safety
  • Uses such as mapping and photography

How a workshop works

Simple on purpose. Most of the planning happens before we arrive.

  1. Talk to us

    Tell us your students' age, group size, time available and what you want them to get out of it.

  2. We plan it

    We propose a topic, schedule and equipment list, and agree the cost with you before anything is booked.

  3. We run it

    We bring the kits and lead the session. Students spend most of the time building, not listening.

  4. We follow up

    We ask for your feedback and share what the students made, so the next workshop is better.
